# Community Code of Conduct

LumeFlow is built for people who want to explain systems clearly. We want the
project community to be equally clear, welcoming, and constructive.

## Our standards

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- Treat people with respect, including when technical opinions differ.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking the person behind them.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce problems and evaluate proposals.
- Respect privacy, licensing, security boundaries, and the confidentiality of vulnerability reports.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sustained disruption, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, Discussions, pull requests,
project events, and other spaces where someone is representing LumeFlow.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/sentimental37). For security vulnerabilities,
use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, or other
participation that violates these standards. Responses will be proportional to
the impact and may include a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
removal from project spaces.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation
against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
